The full export has not yet been reconciled against the report. The web tool cannot parse the XLSX response; a bounded isolated CI probe downloads and inventories the workbook, calculates SHA-256, identifies candidate headers, and retains aggregate audit only. No claim of ingestion or correction status. The export is not equivalent to an open violation list.
