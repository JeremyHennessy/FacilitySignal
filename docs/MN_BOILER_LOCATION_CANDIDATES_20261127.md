# Minnesota DLI boiler location-key pilot, November 27, 2026

Aggregate evidence only. No production release, provider labels, addresses, object IDs, Azure writes, or asset-level matches.

The [Minnesota DLI document page](https://www.dli.mn.gov/workers/boiler-engineer/boiler-documents) describes an updated-daily CSV ZIP. On November 27 UTC, direct HEAD and bounded HTTPS GET returned the pinned 8,082,131-byte ZIP (ETag `"0fd62e75f7edd1:0"`, Last-Modified 11:10:26 UTC). SHA-256, one member CRC and data read through member EOF were verified. The November 26 [prior archival run](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/FacilitySignal/actions/runs/45866627645) screened 171,503 rows and retained an aggregate only; November 27 has the same row count but a different ZIP digest. Do not treat equal counts as identical content.

The live CSV has 31 columns and names different from the older [published dictionary](https://secure.doli.state.mn.us/ccld/data/boilerdatabasedescriptions.pdf). Treat the fresh member's `Asset_Number`, `Location_Record_ID`, and `Milestone` as literal tokens. Asset_Number is complete and unique in this snapshot, not verified stable across editions. The Milestone count includes Active, Scrapped, Out Of Serv, Out of Service and other distinct literals: this is not an in-service roster. We do not map any of those to legal or operating status. Strict streaming cp1252 matches the earlier tested structural decoder; no publisher encoding claim.

The script requires exact source hashes, validates bounded structure, and reports counts only. It checks CMS Minnesota provider rows against DLI **location**, never owner, fields using case/whitespace, exact city and ZIP5, numeric street address, untouched house number and direction, and no separate DLI unit line. The second pass also allows a fixed street-suffix equivalence. Personal, organization and equipment row information is never output; 11 fixtures and count reconciliations passed, and aggregate replay was byte-identical. Raw scratch ZIP was deleted.

Of 473 CMS provider records, 472 were keyable. Literal spelling found 10 candidate address keys, including six with one provider and one DLI location ID. Limited suffix normalization found 116 candidate address keys; 92 had one provider and one DLI location ID, and 90 of those had at least one Boiler-type row in the snapshot. Of the 116, six had multiple provider records, and 20 had multiple or unset DLI location IDs; those groups overlap. These are candidate co-locations, **not confirmed facility equipment**. A normalized key can still be a wrong building, mailing address, or shared campus. Unmatched cases establish no equipment absence. All snapshot statuses are included, so attached Boiler rows do not establish installation, ownership, current use, compliance, or sales demand.

Next: independently review shared-campus and status cases with date and exact site evidence before any site-level release. Do not broaden normalization or publish a provider-to-asset map simply to raise match rates.
