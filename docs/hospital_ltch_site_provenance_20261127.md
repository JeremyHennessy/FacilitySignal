# Hospital and LTCH physical-site evidence gate

The five published-pack dash-only pairs reproduce the existing adjudication at `docs/hospital_ltch_address_adjudication_20261126.json`; this is not a new five-provider discovery. All five remain unverified at the physical-site level.

- The [September 2026 LTCH QRP dictionary, Table 6](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/sites/default/files/data_dictionaries/long_term_care_hospital/LTCH_Data_Dictionary.pdf) defines CCN as the listed facility identifier and keeps Address Lines 1 and 2 separate. It does not define a bare hyphen as missing or give it a normalization rule.
- The [September 29 CMS demographic update](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/quality/long-term-care-hospital-ltch-quality-reporting-program-qrp/how-update-ltch-demographic-data) distinguishes mailing and physical addresses and says the date for universal PECOS sourcing had not been identified. Providers should maintain both systems in the transition. This is not provenance for any specific September 23 directory row; even a matching PECOS address cannot be counted as independent corroboration without establishing lineage.
- The [July 2026 Hospital Enrollments dictionary](https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/Hospital_Enrollments_Data_Dictionary.pdf) defines practice-location address lines and a location-type field; the [Hospital Enrollment guidance, section 3.6](https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/Hospital_Data_Guidance.pdf) says additional practice locations are retrieved via enrollment ID. A correct CCN lookup is therefore only a first step, not proof of a unique or equivalent property.

Replay gate: obtain pinned original LTCH Address Lines 1 and 2 and edition provenance, plus same-edition Hospital Enrollments and Hospital Additional Addresses. Preserve all exact-CCN primary-record enrollments; make the supplemental join by exact enrollment ID, retaining all practice location types without turning missing supplemental data into a zero-location conclusion. If the LTCH projection still lacks verified physical-address scope, or a unique same-site result cannot be established, keep the five as formatting-only candidates. Even verified co-location would not justify merging provider records or transferring their evidence.

No exact enrollment or additional-address rows verified this pass: the public enrollment catalog text surface exposed only a JavaScript shell, and the separate page route was not readable there. This is not an assertion of publisher denial or missing records. No provider identifiers, addresses, raw files, merges, or operational assumptions were retained.
