# CMS national Age Friendly Hospital display

The [official national Timely and Effective Care catalog, page two](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/isrn-hqyy?page=2) shows all six Age Friendly Hospital score-category rows (0–5) for CY 2025 in the November 12, 2026 release. The independent [CMS measure dates catalog](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/4j6d-yzce) agrees on the window. [CMS QualityNet](https://qualitynet.cms.gov/inpatient/iqr/measures) places the measure in Hospital Inpatient Quality Reporting and describes its CY 2025 reporting and FY 2027 payment-determination timing. [CMS measure webinar transcript](https://mmshub.cms.gov/sites/default/files/MMS-OY1-Information-Session-July-2025.transcript.pdf) explains that this is attestation rather than direct patient outcome reporting.

The six displayed scores, ordered by attestation category, sum to 100. The catalog excerpt used here does not independently establish a reporting denominator or a coverage rate, so the output keeps the publisher's values without turning them into hospital counts or saying all hospitals participated. This is a structural measure distribution, not a patient outcome, a facility certification, a current service claim, or proof of any specific institution's performance. No hospital identity or crosswalk is included.

The isolated qualifier validates all six unique keys, same release and window, role/label congruence and displayed numeric fields. Two synthetic tests passed. The stored SHA-256 is for the minimized transient indexed display projection, not original CSV/API bytes. No raw records, public app changes, ingestion, or Azure writes. Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_qualify_cms_age_friendly_display.py`.
