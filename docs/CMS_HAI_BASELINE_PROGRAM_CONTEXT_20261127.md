# FS-040 HAI program and baseline context

The [published FY 2026 final rule, 90 FR 36963–36965](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-08-04/html/2025-14681.htm) separates two public reporting paths. Compare is quarterly, using a rolling four quarters. Its first 2022-standard-population presentation is fall 2026 with all four quarters of CY 2025, which is exactly the period shown by the [November 12 national](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/yd3s-jyhd) and [hospital](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/77hc-ibv8) Care Compare catalog releases observed in the earlier qualification. Baseline 2022 is a rule-supported edition interpretation, not an observed CSV field; the whole provider file still has not been qualified.

The [HAC Reduction Program catalog](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/yq43-i98g) separately displays FY 2026 in its May 13, 2026 edition with a CY 2023–2024 HAI window, not the shorter/different PSI-90 timeframe. [CMS QualityNet](https://qualitynet.cms.gov/inpatient/hac) confirms the program used the 2015 baseline and moves to the 2022 baseline for FY 2028 using CY 2025–2026. Public FY 2028 program results are scheduled for early 2028, not an observed edition. The federal rule and QualityNet distinguish that HAC program schedule from public Care Compare. Neither an overlapping calendar year nor the same six-character provider string justifies merging the programs.

The guard admits only a same-release, same-program, same-model-vintage, same-period and same-measure benchmark-context comparison of the specific observed Care Compare hospital and national releases. A separate structural validator catches cross-program, cross-model and other non-identical period attempts, but does not admit unobserved editions. No risk score, trend or facility association is calculated. A synthetic example holds six observed events constant while predictions of eight versus five yield SIRs of 0.75 versus 1.2. See [CDC's model-vintage discussion](https://www.cdc.gov/nhsn/pdfs/rebaseline/Which-Baseline-Should-I-Use.pdf). A change in SIR across different baselines does not by itself establish a change in facility operations or outcomes.

Five focused tests (this guard and earlier benchmark qualifier) passed. No raw provider records, operational claims, or payment outcomes are retained. No app, Azure, or scheduler change.
