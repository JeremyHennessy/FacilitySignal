# National PECOS dossier linkage guard
November 27, 2026. This change is for the existing local aggregate-only profile, not public Pages and not a new provider crosswalk. [CMS SNF All Owners](https://data.cms.gov/provider-characteristics/hospitals-and-other-facilities/skilled-nursing-facility-all-owners) and [CMS changes of ownership](https://data.cms.gov/provider-characteristics/hospitals-and-other-facilities/skilled-nursing-facility-change-of-ownership) describe distinct source observations; role, report month, and reported change effective date are not interchangeable. Reporting from PECOS is not evidence of property ownership, one physical campus, or current control.

The [six-source pinned PECOS audit](../research/pecos_projection_reconciliation_20261127.json) shows that the retained national enrollment and organization-role sources are November 2026, whereas the Q3 ownership-change files are separate. The previous local profile matched roles on enrollment ID/PAC after a same-CCN/state candidate lookup, but did not compare the two release periods or check whether the same enrollment/PAC also had contradictory provider evidence in the same enrollment source. A synthetic positive match therefore assigned a counted relationship despite cross-state or cross-CCN conflict.

The profile now preserves the enrollment evidence but returns unknown for organization-role linkage across different or unresolved report months. With compatible report months, it excludes role rows whose enrollment/PAC is also associated with a different CCN, a conflicting state, or a missing state in that source family. Repeated identical evidence cannot multiply role rows. These are conservative candidate counts only: a held key may represent legitimate multi-site enrollment, and zero linked rows does not show no owner. Ownership-change, addresses, individual-owner rows, raw data, and public routes are unchanged.

Five in-memory SQLite tests cover matching two period representations, duplicate enrollment rows, missing and conflicting source geography, different families, and malformed or mismatched period. They do not establish live held counts from the private archive; full pinned-database replay and review are required before any public change.
