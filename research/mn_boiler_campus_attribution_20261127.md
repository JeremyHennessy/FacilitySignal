# Minnesota hospital campus attribution controls

Research-only continuation of [the Minnesota boiler candidate status audit](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/FacilitySignal/commit/618a9303a4da5406b10edad3304eb6b5128a7bde). These fixtures use public operator addresses and artificial examples. They do not inspect or expose equipment records, link any asset to a campus, or establish coverage.

The [Minnesota Department of Health HCCIS facility list notes](https://www2cdn.web.health.state.mn.us/data/economics/hccis/facilities.html) distinguish cessation of independent reporting from physical closure: a listed facility can continue as an offsite campus of another licensed hospital. Treat reporting status, physical status and equipment status independently, with source dates. A provider record at one address is not a roster of its campuses. Thus the 92 one-provider/one-DLI-location candidate address keys (81 with a literal Active boiler row) describe local match cardinality only, not completeness of a provider's campus list or currently operating equipment.

Two negative controls exercise the existing candidate address key. The [Fairview specialty directory](https://www.mhealthfairview.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/Specialty_Directory.pdf) distinguishes West Bank at 2450 Riverside Avenue from East Bank at 500 Harvard Street SE. [Mayo's Rochester campus directory](https://college.mayo.edu/campus-and-community/minnesota/) lists Saint Marys at 1216 Second Street SW and Methodist at 201 W. Center Street, in the same ZIP5. In both cases a shared organization name must not transfer any candidate to the other campus. These directories alone do not establish federal certification for both sites. A synthetic reporting-closure fixture checks that separate site keys remain distinct without treating reporting status as an address or site-closure flag; it does not test a live MDH import.

Run from the repository root: `python scripts/test_research_mn_boiler_campus_attribution_20261127.py`.

Before widening coverage, test an independently dated site roster against any inferred provider-to-campus crosswalk. Missing site coverage should remain unknown. Preserve second-line building details as unresolved, and keep any reviewed campus links separate from asset attribution, present operating status, ownership and compliance. No Azure, Pages or data pipeline changes accompany this note.
